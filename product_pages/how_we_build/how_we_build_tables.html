Direct Code Sourcing

Our Tables are rooted in official zoning codes: the texts and maps published by local jurisdictions. We don’t draw from assessors’ maps, third-party aggregated data, or other unreliable, unofficial sources.

Human Verification

Our dataset is created, verified, and updated by human experts, not AI. Every entry undergoes a multi-step review process by team members with expertise spanning law, geography, planning, and data science.

Regular Update Cycles

Recognizing the value of refreshed zoning data, we continuously monitor, track, and input updates, and we regularly conduct full-state and full-metro area reviews.
