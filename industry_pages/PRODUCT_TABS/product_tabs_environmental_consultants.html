Rasters standardize residential and nonresidential uses into 30×30m grid cells. From agriculture to utilities to mining, Rasters stack directly with land cover, habitat, and hydrology geospatial data, making zoning one more input to your analysis.

Compare candidate sites with Zoning Slices

Slices flatten overlapping base and overlay districts into non-overlapping polygons, each carrying exactly one set of prevailing rules. Once a shortlist is down to a handful of sites, Slices show what’s allowed on each parcel, facilitating site selection before anyone goes into the field.
